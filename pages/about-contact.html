ABOUT THE AEGIS NETWORK

Chartered in 2024 to bridge civilian emergency services with registered superhuman operatives through ethical oversight and zero-latency communication.

SHIELDING HUMANITY THROUGH VERIFIED ALLIANCES

When rogue meta-humans, planetary catastrophes, and runaway rogue AI threaten metropolitan civil infrastructure, traditional first responders often face insurmountable firepower disparities.

The Aegis Help Portal was established as an open civilian transponder grid. Using decentralized tactical sensors and algorithmic dispatch routing, we match distress signals with operatives equipped with the exact counter-abilities required—minimizing property damage and neutralizing catastrophic casualties in minutes.
